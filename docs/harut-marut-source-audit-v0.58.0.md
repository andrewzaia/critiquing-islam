# CI-047 evidence audit - v0.58.0

Date: 2026-11-10

## Precise claim

Quran 2:102 identifies Harut and Marut at Babylon as two angels who warn pupils before teaching them knowledge used to separate a husband and wife. It acquits Solomon of disbelief, condemns devils who teach magic, and conditions the reported harm on divine permission.

## Quran-first controls

- Quran 2:102: https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=2&verse=102
- Syntax and al-malakayni: https://corpus.quran.com/treebank.jsp?chapter=2&verse=102
- Immediate moral context, Quran 2:103: https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=2&verse=103
- Solomon and supernatural workers: Quran 21:82 and 34:12
- Angels who do not disobey, in context: Quran 66:6

The Quranic passage does not call the pair fallen angels or narrate Venus, drunkenness or punishment in a well. These are later traditions. The text attributes warning and instruction to the same pair and does not describe a historical witness or date of the event.

## Dated and comparative witnesses

- Genesis 6:1-4: https://www.sefaria.org/Genesis.6.1-4 . Brief pre-Islamic Biblical passage, not a magic lesson.
- Book of Watchers, 1 Enoch 6 and 8: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Book_of_Enoch_%28Charles%29/Chapter_08 . Fallen heavenly beings teach dangerous arts; their names and setting differ from Quran 2:102.
- Aramaic Enoch from Qumran: https://www.nli.org.il/en/books/NNL_ALEPH997009971868305171/NLI . Pre-Islamic physical attestations of Enoch tradition, not a complete early copy of today's translated Book of Enoch.
- Critical text reader: https://pseudepigrapha.org/reader.html?doc=1En.xml . Distinguish edited reconstructions and witnesses.
- Jubilees 10:7-14: https://adfonteslibrary.org/work-st-pseudepigrapha-jubilees . Heavenly instruction is framed as protective.
- Josephus, Antiquities 8.44-49: https://lexundria.com/j_aj/8.44-49/wst . First-century CE witness to stories about Solomonic exorcistic knowledge, not a witness to Solomon's life.
- Iranian name hypotheses: https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/harut-and-marut/ and https://www.iranicaonline.org/articles/hordad/ . Proposed linguistic connections do not demonstrate a specific narrative transmission chain.

## Verdict and limits

[Verified] Pre-Islamic sources contain stories of heavenly instruction and Solomonic spirit practices. [Verified] The surviving Quranic wording names two angels at Babylon and describes a warning before instruction. [Likely] Shared motifs reflect engagement with an existing narrative environment. The precise literary intermediary and the supernatural Babylonian event remain unverified.

Do not infer direct borrowing simply from priority; do not infer a historical miracle from narrative appearance in scripture. Earlier copies attest words, not angels. The 24-section article includes 20 distinct source links. The structural checks can test navigation, citation links and minimum depth but cannot independently verify the miraculous event.
