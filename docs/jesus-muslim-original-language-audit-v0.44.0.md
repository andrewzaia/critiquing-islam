# CI-009 original-language and historical audit - v0.44.0

## Main claims
Quran 3:52:20 and 5:111:13 identify the disciples as *muslimuna*. Quran 3:67:10 describes Abraham as *musliman*. The [Quranic Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com/) confirms form IV active participles. The Quran also asserts continuity of submission across earlier prophets in 3:19 and 2:132-136. These are Quranic claims, not independently proven first-century speeches.

## Source chronology
The Greek of [Mark 12:29](https://openbible.com/text/mark/12-29.htm) places the Shema in Jesus' teaching. Galatians 4:4 describes Jesus as born under the law. Galatians 1:18-19 and 2:9-14 report Paul's contacts and dispute with early Jerusalem leaders. Acts 21 is later narrative, not another first-century eyewitness.

P46, Codex Vaticanus and Codex Sinaiticus establish pre-Islamic transmission of Christian texts; they do not prove each narrated event. Garrick V. Allen, [Titles in the New Testament Papyri](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/new-testament-studies/article/titles-in-the-new-testament-papyri/4ADE3BD7541916BEA44E4E2067AE66A7) (2022), discusses the scribal transmission of P46. Modern interpretive context comes from Yonatan Adler, [Was Jesus a Jew?](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/2024/02/19/was-jesus-a-jew/) (2024) and Paula Fredriksen, [When Christians Were Jews](https://yalebooks.yale.edu/book/9780300240740/when-christians-were-jews/) (2018). Both are modern scholars, not ancient witnesses.

## Competing explanations
Universal submission is a real Quranic theological claim, but does not independently establish Jesus' historical doctrine. A lost original message matching the Quran's distinct Christology is a possible hypothesis that lacks a securely dated early text and demonstrated transmission chain. Early Jewish Christian continuity has earlier surviving documentary evidence, without proving the supernatural truth of all Christian claims.

**Text-first verdict:** the Quran does attribute submission to the disciples. **Historical verdict:** Jesus belongs to the first-century Jewish world; the Quran's later specific classification is not independently attested in the evidence considered. **Biblical theological verdict:** the Quranic correction conflicts with the apostolic proclamation under the site's declared Biblical-Christian premises.

## Falsification and limitations
A securely dated, independently corroborating pre-Islamic witness conveying Jesus' distinctive alleged teaching, plus a verifiable alteration history, could alter the historical verdict. Repeated late assertions are not substitutes for evidence. Automated checks validate links, section structure and citation coverage, not supernatural events or full manuscript collation.
