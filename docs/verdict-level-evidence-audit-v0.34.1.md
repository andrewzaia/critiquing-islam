# Evidence-weighted verdict review - v0.34.1

## Scope and burden of proof

A verdict-level editorial review was conducted across all 39 published investigations and the unnumbered research guide. It inspected the existing conclusions for unsupported harmonisations, failure to prioritise source evidence, and genuine interpretive uncertainties. This was not a fresh line-by-line philological review or a physical collation of manuscripts.

Apply the **balance of probabilities**, not proof beyond reasonable doubt. Choose the best-supported explanation rather than merely listing possibilities. An unevidenced rescue hypothesis must not receive equal weight to directly attested wording and earlier documents. A qualified judgement is still appropriate where the original language or chronology establishes a genuine unresolved question.

## What was strengthened

- **CI-038:** The text now separates the independent historical evaluation from the conditional Christian theological evaluation. If the earlier apostolic gospel is authoritative and a supernatural visitor is assumed, deceptive agency better fits a contrary gospel than a faithful angel of God. Galatians 1:8 concerns a contradictory message, not the physical pressing itself. No independent proof identifies Satan as a historical visitor.
- **CI-008:** The dual Aaron/Imran naming correspondence is documented; an imagined first-century family genealogy has no comparable independent attestation.
- **CI-011:** The principal biblical prophecy claims fail on their original wording; an unspecified lost prediction is not positive counterevidence.
- **CI-015:** Undocumented Jewish sects and alternative identities for Uzayr do not displace the extant record by logical possibility alone.
- **Methodology:** Added a formal comparative-evidence standard, disclosure of theological premises, and the requirement to rank theories without invented probability percentages.

## Why some limits remain

Documentary silence does not prove that a tiny lost group could never have existed. A late monetary word does not automatically specify minted coins, and the name al-Samiri alone does not secure a Samaritan identity. These are substantive limits of source language and historical evidence, not permissions to treat unattested explanations as persuasive. The correct conclusion can be definite about a *failed historical proof* while not claiming that every conceivable scenario has been disproved.

## Validation limits

Automated checks can guard source links, section structure, prescribed research verdicts and navigation; they do not establish the truth of supernatural experiences, independently collate ancient manuscripts or substantiate every theological assertion. The Quran browser, Qira'at data and Quran.Foundation integration are unrelated to this editorial release.
