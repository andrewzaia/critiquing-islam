# Manuscript catalogue evidence classifications - v0.71.0

## Scope

The 68 permanent records from v0.70.0 and nine manuscript groups are retained. This release classifies the existing descriptions; it is not a new collation of manuscript images, independent verification of every reading, or an estimate of the Quran's overall variation rate. The existing primary classification and evidence status remain available.

## Independent dimensions

| Semantic impact | Records | Definition |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Spelling / name form | 3 | Same referent, including spelling associated with pronunciation |
| Limited wording or grammatical effect | 25 | Local repair, clarification, emphasis or broadly equivalent proposition |
| Meaning or referent differs | 31 | Reported local lexical, grammatical or referent difference |
| Impact unresolved | 9 | Insufficient earlier wording or context to assign an effect |

Thirteen records have inferred, partial or unknown readings. An inferred form can have an assessable conditional semantic effect. A published reading remains an attributed scholarly report. Neither a semantic difference nor a legal or theological context alone establishes a changed law or doctrine.

| Canonical status | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| Canonical / regional parallel | 8 |
| Noncanonical literary parallel | 7 |
| Unrecorded in cited survey | 5 |
| Not established here | 48 |

The canonical axis records explicit parallels already described in the linked investigations. MV-056 is classified as a noncanonical literary parallel because Cellard identifies the reading with Abu al-Aliya. MV-050 and MV-053 are regional alignment cases, independently of their physical insertion classification. The unassessed category is not an assertion that the remaining readings are outside the ten canonical traditions.

| Physical state | Records |
| --- | ---: |
| Reported insertion | 17 |
| Reported amendment | 7 |
| Reported erasure / overwrite | 6 |
| No intervention established | 38 |

Physical state describes the reported intervention, not its date, cause or certainty. Inferred corrections retain the inferred evidence status. No intervention established does not mean unchanged.

Context assignments: 12 legal, 30 theological and 26 general language/narrative. These are navigation labels for context, not findings of changed doctrine or law.

## Repeated verse comparisons

- 2:196: MV-002, MV-003 and MV-004 are observations from the Sanaa Stanford leaf. MV-003 and MV-004 concern the same line. These are not three independent witnesses.
- 10:22: MV-011 (Parisino), MV-057 (Amrensis) and MV-061 (Topkapi). The Parisino and Topkapi verb comparisons must be distinguished from the Amrensis correction of a different word at the end of the verse.

The static comparison contains the existing snippets and permanent record links, and remains available without JavaScript. Its filter button clears other filters and selects the verse. The number of manuscript groups is not a count of independent textual ancestors.

## Manuscript access

The existing Corpus Coranicum resource for Arabe 328(a), [f. 10v](https://corpuscoranicum.org/en/manuscripts/13/page/10v?sura=4&verse=11), supplies an indexed line transcription including 4:12, line 18. The reported earlier state is not independently recovered by this transcription. The [Tübingen Ma VI 165 f. 1r locator](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts/107/page/1r) remains linked; opening it returned a JavaScript shell, so fresh collation was not performed. These are page locators, not newly verified facsimile images.

Other previously linked resources for MV-006, MV-007, MV-015, MV-020, MV-025 and MV-039 are explicitly marked related resources rather than verified exact folios. The underlying citations remain attached. No new image or folio URL is asserted without matching evidence. Direct image comparison remains a research priority.

## Validation scope

Regression checks cover the complete record set, facet totals, combined filters, both repeated-verse groups and evidence boundaries. These checks establish structural consistency, not independent scholarly validation or cross-device browser testing. The Quran explorer and research investigation count remain unchanged.
