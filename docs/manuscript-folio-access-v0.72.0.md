# Manuscript folio access audit - v0.72.0

## Scope and method

Thirteen existing catalogue records have received additional institutional access links or more precise folio locations. No new variant is asserted and no uncertain reading has been promoted to a secure reading merely because an archive has digitised a codex.

## Tübingen Ma VI 165

- [University Library OpenDigi record](https://opendigi.ub.uni-tuebingen.de/opendigi/MaVI165), DOI 10.20345/digitue.1731: official shelfmark, digitised page ranges and public-domain notice checked.
- [Corpus Coranicum, f. 1r](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts/107/page/1r): indexed transcription attributed to Salome Beridze, line 14, displays the explicitly pointed ta of the second-person plural at 17:43. This supports the reported comparison in MV-035. This is a transcription cross-check, not a fresh reading of the photograph or a dated ink analysis.
- [Islamic Awareness, Tübingen contents index](https://www.islamic-awareness.org/quran/text/mss/tubingen): secondary verse-to-folio map. The relevant rows place 17:90 within 17:82-93 on f. 3v, 19:9 within 19:7-20 on f. 11v, and 29:31 within 29:27-34 on f. 56r.

## Birmingham Islamic Arabic 1572a

- [Cadbury Research Library's official image album](https://www.flickr.com/photos/cadburyresearchlibrary/albums/72157655161018888) identifies the four current pages and includes a separate image with surah references. The [University FAQ](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/cadbury/birmingham-quran-mingana-collection/birmingham-quran/faqs) permits downloading for private study and requires contacting Imaging Services for publication. This release links to the source without reproducing the photographs.
- [Fedeli's 2020 XML dataset](https://research.birmingham.ac.uk/en/datasets/quranic-transcriptions-of-mingana-manuscripts/) identifies the underlying transcriptions, DOI 10.25500/edata.bham.00000530. [The 2015 project repository](https://epapers.bham.ac.uk/id/eprint/1964/) supplies the project description. Attempts to retrieve the XML and the DOI destination failed.
- [Corpus Coranicum f. 1v](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts/2278/page/1v?sura=20&verse=1) displays a reference-text panel covering 20:13-40. This is explicitly the modern Cairo edition, not the manuscript transcription. It is useful as a location cross-check for MV-007, not proof of the amended reading.
- [Official photograph record for current f. 1v](https://www.flickr.com/photos/cadburyresearchlibrary/18871751093/in/album-72157655161018888) identifies that side of the manuscript.

MV-006 and MV-007 retain Fedeli's original combined folio references and scholarly evidence states. Present foliation must not be assumed identical to the original combined foliation. Neither reading has been freshly collated from the photographs.

## Parisino-petropolitanus

[Gallica, BnF Arabe 328](https://gallica.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/btv1b8415207g) is the verified digitisation entry for the shelfmark, confirmed by the [Biblissima record](https://iiif.biblissima.fr/collections/manifest/3b400e3a00dbc2ec404e9b45bbc0f339f4300c1a). MV-008 through MV-014 concern part (a), not all parts of Arabe 328. No unverified Gallica image-number mapping is generated. Direct Corpus Coranicum folio requests returned errors or a JavaScript shell.

The indexed [Corpus Coranicum .org f. 47v catalogue](https://corpuscoranicum.org/en/manuscripts/13/page/47v) reports 640-680 CE, whereas the indexed [.de f. 1v catalogue](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts/13/page/1v) reports 750 CE. Both describe the same 56-folio shelfmark and supply no radiocarbon result. The difference is not resolved by this review. The catalogue now displays both estimates rather than treating one as a laboratory result or combining them into a measured interval.

## Verification limits

One indexed transcription cross-check and three secondary folio-location cross-checks were completed. Institutional access pages were checked, but original images, complete facsimiles and XML payloads were not retrieved for independent collation. All 68 existing records, their reading states and permanent identifiers remain unchanged. The underlying research now includes the institutional access citations and date comparison. Automated checks establish structural completeness only, not scholarly confirmation of the handwritten marks.
