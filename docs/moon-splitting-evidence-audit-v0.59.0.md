# CI-049 - Moon splitting source and evidence audit (v0.59.0)

## Scope and direct textual evidence

- Primary Arabic: Quran 54:1 reads `اقْتَرَبَتِ السَّاعَةُ وَانْشَقَّ الْقَمَرُ`. The Quranic Arabic Corpus parses `inshaqqa` as a form VII third-person masculine perfect verb. The Hour is near and the Moon is described as split; the text gives neither a date nor named observers.
- Immediate context: Quran 54:2 describes people rejecting a sign as magic; 54:3-5 continues with rejection and warning. This supports a traditional past-sign reading without turning the text into an independent sighting record.
- Comparative Quranic constraints: 17:59, 29:50-51, 17:90-93 and 21:5-6 discuss signs demanded or withheld. They create questions for a proposed spectacular public demonstration but do not establish an unavoidable formal contradiction.
- Serious alternative: a future-eschatological interpretation of 54:1 is recognised in Andreas Goerke's published research and is relevant to the approaching Hour, but must address the sign language of verse 2.

## Chronology and limits

1. Alleged event: located in the Meccan preaching period by the historical interpretation, without a secure astronomical date in the received verse.
2. Quranic wording: canonical textual witness to an assertion, not an independent observation log.
3. Hadith collection: Bukhari and Muslim assembled recognised collections in the ninth century; alleged earlier chains need separate testing.
4. Manuscripts: particular surviving copies witness transmitted wording, not the independent physical event.
5. Malabar Qiṣṣa: Ines Weinrich's 2026 article compares two differing London manuscript narratives. The extant story's stage of development is not securely dated to Muhammad's lifetime, so it cannot be promoted to a seventh-century independent observation.

## Science and failed proof

NASA documents lunar graben as faults formed under extensional stress. The Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter Camera identifies Rima Ariadaeus as a local geological structure, not a global reassembly seam. An AFP report quotes NASA lunar scientist Brad Bailey rejecting claims that the scientific record shows split-and-reassembly. This directly rebuts modern assertions of NASA confirmation. It does not experimentally exclude every hypothetical transient supernatural appearance that would leave no physical trace.

## Evidence verdict and revision criteria

- **Text [Verified]:** The perfect verb and rejected-sign context are genuinely present.
- **History [Verified on reviewed records]:** Multiple later companion attributions exist, but the cited sources do not establish independent contemporary astronomical verification.
- **Physical science [Verified]:** Published lunar rille evidence does not document an ancient split and reassembly.
- **Biblical theological comparison:** Deuteronomy 13 and Galatians 1 distinguish signs from doctrinal authority; Matthew 12 and 1 Corinthians 15 are identified comparative primary texts, not automatically authenticated miracles.
- **Would change the historical verdict:** securely dated, independently sourced early observation, reliable provenance and a checkable fit to the alleged phenomenon; for a geological claim, an accepted global physical finding rather than a local trench.
- **Editorial limits:** structural source checks do not independently validate a historical miracle, astronomical visibility or supernatural causation. The Quran explorer, readings and API are outside this milestone.
