At 3:133 and 5:53 the alignment towards Iraqi regional forms is evidence for modification between coherent traditions. At 10:22 the difference is at the end of the verse, not the earlier Parisino verb variant.

## Evidential boundaries

The monumental attribution claims lack demonstrated provenance to Uthman; this does not date the underlying textual tradition to the age of the surviving copies. Correction can reflect copying error or deliberate alignment without establishing an alternative prophetic original. The selected comparisons are not a statistical sample. Further direct folio collation, especially of Samarkand, is the next priority.
