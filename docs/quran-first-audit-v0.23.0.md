# Quran-first textual evidence audit - v0.23.0

Date: 2026-11-04

## Scope

A repository-wide triage reviewed the published research library for wording that might allow later hadith or tafsir to replace the Quranic statement under scrutiny. The scan used interpretive-language patterns to prioritise attention; it was not a semantic proof or a complete line-by-line historical verification of every article.

Four studies were revised in depth: Quran 65:4 and marriage age, reward to Jews and Christians, no compulsion versus jizya, and Quranic preservation. The existing primary-source hierarchy remains documented on the methodology page.

## Direct checks

- Quran 65:4, https://quran.com/65/4 : states the waiting-period category for those who have not menstruated without a numeric minimum age. Quranic Arabic Corpus translations, https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=65&verse=4 , show why translator additions must be kept separate from the Arabic.
- Quran 33:49, https://quran.com/33/49 : separately exempts a specified category of divorce from the waiting period. This is a legal cross-reference, not evidence of any particular historical marriage.
- Quran 2:62, https://quran.com/2/62 , Quran 5:69, https://quran.com/5/69 , and Quran 3:85, https://quran.com/3/85 : distinguish the written reward conditions and exclusive acceptance claim from later proposed chronological restrictions.
- Quran 2:256, https://quran.com/2/256 , and Quran 9:29, https://quran.com/9/29 : distinguish the refusal of religious compulsion from the political obligations and exceptions discussed in their broader setting.
- Quran 2:106, https://quran.com/2/106 : provides a general statement about replacement or forgetting, not a named historical inventory of omitted recitation.

## Source priority

1. Reproduce or paraphrase Quranic wording and context accurately.
2. Test linguistic distinctions and compare relevant verses.
3. Use independent historical records and manuscripts to test historical claims.
4. Treat hadith as transmitted allegations and evidence of subsequent reception, rather than independent verification of the alleged event.
5. Treat tafsir as historical interpretation, not a binding addition to the Quran.

Counterarguments are tested by the same rules regardless of whether they help or challenge a critical conclusion.

## Findings and limits

Quran 65:4 does not supply an explicit adults-only age restriction; the article does not infer from that absence that a particular marriage took place or that the Quran commands marriage at a fixed age. For salvation and jizya the articles distinguish genuine textual tensions from strict logical contradictions, which require conflicting propositions about the same case and conditions. For preservation, manuscript agreement and variation are evaluated separately from hadith claims and theological explanations.

Automated checks validate the presence of crucial sections, citations and curated findings. They do not certify the Arabic philology, textual history, moral reasoning or any underlying supernatural claim.
