# CI-037 evidence audit - v0.33.0

Date: 2026-11-05

## Question and conclusion

**Question:** What can be established about the relationship between Quran 5:32 and the earlier maxim in Mishnah Sanhedrin 4:5?

The article distinguishes three questions: (1) whether the passages show a demonstrable distinctive parallel, (2) whether the Jewish literary formulation was recorded before the Quran, and (3) whether a particular act of transmission or copying can be reconstructed.

- **Directly established:** both texts place a one-life/whole-world or all-humanity analogy after Cain and Abel. Quran 5:32 presents the saying as a decree given to the Children of Israel, with explicit conditions for retribution and corruption.
- **Strong historical inference:** the earlier rabbinic teaching formed part of the Mishnah's compiled literary tradition centuries before Islam; the Quran engages with this recognisable Jewish teaching.
- **Not established by the cited evidence:** which rabbi, oral performance, written copy or precise linguistic intermediary gave the maxim to a seventh-century Quranic audience, or an absolute reconstruction of the original wording of every Mishnah witness.
- **Failed narrow claim:** the life/world analogy was a unique formulation first introduced by the Quran. This does not decide an untestable assertion about whether later revelation can reconfirm an earlier truth.

## Manuscript and date qualifications

The Mishnah is generally dated to compilation around 190-230 CE. This dates the literary tradition, **not** each letter in a surviving medieval manuscript. Its most studied complete manuscript witnesses were copied much later. https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.4.5?lang=bi&with=About and https://www.lib.cam.ac.uk/collections/departments/taylor-schechter-genizah-research-unit/fragment-month/fotm-2023/fragment-4

The expression commonly translated as 'of Israel' is present in some versions of Mishnah Sanhedrin 4:5 and absent in others, including witnesses discussed in the Hebrew manuscript commentary. Claims of definite original restriction or unrestricted universality require controlled textual criticism, not selection of a preferred modern translation. https://www.sefaria.org/German_Commentary_on_Mishnah_Sanhedrin.4.5 and https://www.sefaria.org/Mishnah_Sanhedrin.4.5?lang2=he&with=Commentary

Mishnah Sanhedrin 4:5 is the standard reference used in the site's primary links. Koller (2025) refers to the corresponding passage as 4:12 under another numbering tradition; this is not another independent ancient witness. https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/6653ba4b-458f-4d98-a8b3-f03bde647e2f

## Scholarly interpretations and limits

Michael Pregill (2022) situates the maxim within a much wider net of Quranic and rabbinic narrative connections and disputes simplistic identification of the raven scene with a specific alleged midrashic original: https://lockwoodonlinejournals.com/index.php/jiqsa/article/view/2057

Aaron Koller (2025) treats Quran 5:32 as a case of engagement with Mishnah material in Arabia: https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/items/6653ba4b-458f-4d98-a8b3-f03bde647e2f

Pregill (2023) warns against one uniform model for the diverse ways the Quran repurposes Jewish and Christian traditions: https://online.ucpress.edu/SLA/article-abstract/7/4/516/197737/From-the-Mishnah-to-Mu-ammadJewish-Traditions-of

These studies are cited for scholarly argument; they are not counted as extra primary manuscripts. Their publicly accessible abstracts or metadata do not justify claiming that full paywalled articles have been inspected when they have not.

## Scope and acceptance

The published article has 19 analytical sections, 23 separate source entries, a textual comparison matrix, and two distinct verdicts. CI checks verify numbered reference anchors are all used, that study sections and navigation are present, and that the homepage, research library and source register link to it.

This is structural and internal-link verification. It does **not** establish the historical truth of the Cain and Abel episode, test every external webpage live, collate all Hebrew manuscripts, or resolve the authorship and supernatural origin of the Quran. Those topics require separate evidence. No Quran explorer, Qira'at corpus, Quran.Foundation integration, API or Cloudflare configuration changes are part of this release.
