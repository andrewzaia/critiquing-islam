The later substitution of al-Mamun's name in the foundation inscription is evidence of patron-name alteration, not a finding of a Quranic verse alteration. The repeated coin design is not two separate Quranic textual ancestors. The matching chapter on the monument and abbreviation on coins favour contextual selection rather than an absent Quranic verse.

## Scope and verdict

CI-003 now examines the dated material evidence and tests the claim of late textual circulation separately from absolute preservation. Explicitly contextualised quotations do not establish a competing continuous text. The two material dating records are not exact dates of writing. The 68 manuscript comparisons remain unchanged; no documentary adaptations are counted as additional manuscript variants. Codex Ma'il is the already-covered Or. 2165, not a new independent group.

No original artefact or complete facsimile was independently collated. The next priority is located regional consonantal evidence from continuous texts and direct image comparison where accessible.
