## Arrangement finding

Karimi-Nia describes rewritten transitions at A49v-A50r, A104v-A105r and A59v-A60r; A82v-A83r supplies evidence of erasure at the start of surah 10. The seven terminal leaves B123r-B129v are replacements. Intact boundaries A98v and B122r are among those consistent with the proposed Ibn Masud sequence. The hypothesis follows from the pattern of interventions and comparison with later order lists. It does not recover every erased transition or establish personal ownership by Ibn Masud. The source's conclusion is that the base text is predominantly Uthmanic.

## Verdict

Physical manuscript uniformity fails the reported comparisons. Broad textual continuity is compatible with copying variation and changed chapter order. Neither the changes nor later correction proves an alternative revealed text or dates the intervention to Uthman's committee. Further original-image collation and independently dated comparanda would strengthen or revise the findings.
