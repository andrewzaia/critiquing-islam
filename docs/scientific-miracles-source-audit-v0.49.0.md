# CI-033 - Scientific miracle claims in the Quran
Original-language, historical and natural-science evidence audit for v0.49.0, 8 November 2026.

## Precise proposition
A scientific miracle requires more than modern compatibility. The Quranic passage must, from its own original-language context and without importing later science, express a reasonably precise physical claim unavailable through ordinary pre-Islamic observation or intellectual traditions. A dated interpretation made before a finding is stronger evidence than a retrospective match.

## Cosmological texts
- [Quran 21:30, Arabic Corpus](https://corpus.quran.com/wordbyword.jsp?chapter=21&verse=30) uses a paired heaven-and-earth subject, dual *kanata*, *ratqan* (joined or closed) and *fataqnahuma* (separated them). The sentence contains no cosmic background radiation, numerical chronology or predictive expansion law.
- [Genesis 1:6-10](https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=Genesis%201%3A6-10&version=KJV) is a pre-Islamic creation account involving cosmic waters being divided. The motif's antiquity is independently relevant; it does not prove direct transmission or identical original meaning.
- [Quran 51:47, lexical search](https://corpus.quran.com/search.jsp?q=lem%3AmuwsiE&s=3) documents the form IV active participle *musi'* used in 51:47 and in 2:236 for a wealthy or capable person. A physical expansion reading remains linguistically possible, but a Hubble-law prediction is not unique.
- [Quran 41:11](https://corpus.quran.com/wordbyword.jsp?chapter=41&verse=11) uses *dukhan*, smoke, and the narrative includes seven heavens. Analogy with hot primordial plasma does not identify measurable modern plasma dynamics or seven observable cosmic strata.
- [NASA, Big Bang and the Evolution of the Universe](https://science.nasa.gov/astrophysics/programs/physics-of-the-cosmos/big-bang-and-the-evolution-of-the-universe/) describes the cosmic microwave background and empirically constrained model. Observations are not inferred from scripture.

## Pre-Islamic medicine and biology
- [Corpus Coranicum, Galen De Semine I.8](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/verse-navigator/sura/23/verse/13/intertexts/986) presents an ancient staged account of embryonic formation predating Islam, with precise scholarly Greek-edition provenance and translation.
- [Digital Corpus for Graeco-Arabic Studies, De foetuum formatione](https://www.graeco-arabic-studies.org/view/gal.defoetform-orig-gr1) presents the original Greek of an ancient medical treatise. The ancient composition date must not be confused with the modern edition or surviving manuscript date.
- [Quran 76:2](https://corpus.quran.com/translation.jsp?chapter=76&verse=2) describes a mingled drop, but modern translator additions regarding sperm and egg require independent linguistic support.
- [NCBI male genitourinary anatomy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK562291/) documents the testes, epididymis, accessory glands and ejaculation pathway; a literal adult semen-source claim between backbone and ribs would not match this reproductive anatomy. An embryo-migration alternative is different from the anatomy of semen production.
- Original Quranic 23:14's staged terms are not microscopic labels, and contemporary skeletal and muscular differentiation includes overlapping processes.

## Geology and hydrology
- [Quran 16:15](https://corpus.quran.com/wordbyword.jsp?chapter=16&verse=15) uses *rawasiya* and *tamida* for firm mountains and the earth swaying. [British Geological Survey](https://www.bgs.ac.uk/discovering-geology/earth-hazards/earthquakes/what-causes-earthquakes/) describes mountain-forming plate collisions and associated faults, not mountains as devices preventing tectonic motion.
- [Quran 78:7](https://corpus.quran.com/treebank.jsp?chapter=78&verse=7) likens mountains to *awtad*, pegs. This may be a vivid image and some mountain ranges have real crustal roots, but the verse does not predict isostasy, fault rates or seismic suppression.
- [Quran 55:20](https://corpus.quran.com/wordmorphology.jsp?location=%2855%3A20%3A2%29) and [25:53](https://corpus.quran.com/wordbyword.jsp?chapter=25&verse=53) use *barzakh* for a barrier in water contexts. [NOAA](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/education/tutorial_estuaries/est01_whatis.html) documents real mixing and variable salinity where rivers meet the sea. A stratified boundary can exist without being impermeable.

## Different evidentiary findings
**Textual:** the cited terms are genuine, with ambiguities and poetic imagery to be acknowledged.
**Scientific:** astronomy, anatomy, geology and hydrology provide independently measured physical explanations; a general correspondence is not sufficient proof of a scientific miracle, and some rigid literal mappings conflict with observation.
**Historical:** earlier medical and cosmological concepts predate Islam; similarity alone is not proof of textual borrowing.
**Theological:** a Biblical-Christian judgement about revelation cannot be replaced by either a scientific compatibility claim or an unsupported assertion of total scientific error.

## What could change the verdict
A documented interpretation made before scientific confirmation which unambiguously predicts a novel physical phenomenon and distinguishes it from alternative explanations would materially strengthen the miracle claim. A demonstrated unambiguous physical statement incompatible with observation would strengthen a particular error verdict. This audit does not establish every historical transmission pathway or adjudicate supernatural inspiration.
