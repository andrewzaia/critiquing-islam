# CI-003 folio-level evidence audit - v0.62.0

## Witnesses and method

Compare the modern Hafs wording with located published manuscript readings, not a reconstructed full alternative Quran. Relative omissions do not determine chronological priority. Multiple studies of one folio are not independent manuscripts.

- [Sadeghi and Goudarzi, Der Islam 87 (2012), pp. 1-129](https://doi.org/10.1515/islam-2011-0025), Table 3 and [published transcription, pp. 44-45](https://bible-quran.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/Sadeghi-Goudarzi-sana-Origins-of-the-Quran.pdf). The Stanford 2007 recto and verso are the lower-text loci studied. Rendered edition pages 44-45 were inspected.
- [Sadeghi and Bergmann, Arabica 57 (2010), pp. 343-436](https://doi.org/10.1163/157005810X504518), Table 2, Appendix 1 p. 418, Appendix 2 pp. 422-423 and Appendix 4. The accessible [published copy](https://bible-quran.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/Sadeghi-Bergmann-The-Codex-of-a-Companion-of-the-Prophet-and-the-quran-of-the-Prophet.pdf) supplies the comparisons. Extracted text was checked, but rendering the relevant appendix pages failed.
- [Fedeli (2015), thesis, pp. 147-182](https://etheses.bham.ac.uk/id/eprint/5864/1/Fedeli15PhD.pdf), especially the table on pp. 171-172. Extracted table text was accessible; an attempted page rendering failed. Claims are attributed to the published examination, not a new collation of the manuscript.
- [Birmingham manuscript FAQ](https://www.birmingham.ac.uk/facilities/cadbury/birmingham-quran-mingana-collection/birmingham-quran/faqs) and [Corpus Coranicum catalogue](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts/2278/page/1v?sura=20&verse=1). Distinguish the original combined folio numbering from the present two-leaf catalogue, and the university's parchment range from the later calibration.

The snippets omit uncertain neighbouring letters. They are not full diplomatic transcriptions. The legal text in 2:196 explicitly enumerates fewer options in the lower text. That observation survives an accidental-copying hypothesis, while the claim of intentional suppression would require additional evidence. The Birmingham conjunction is a reported amended form, not necessarily the final text.

## Limits and follow-up

Marcel 2 f. 30v at Quran 30:9 has an unreadable erasure, not an identified equivalent of the Sanaa omitted charity expression. See the [Marcel audit](marcel-2-quran-30-9-evidence-audit-v0.61.0.md). Birmingham 1572a is associated with Arabe 328(c), while Arabe 328(a)-(b) belongs to a distinct codex. Do not count all three as the same physical manuscript.

Priority for a subsequent release: locate additional Marcel and Parisino-petropolitanus readings in critical editions, compare their folio references and classify their direction and correction state. No such additional readings are claimed here. Direct imaging, a full collation, an estimate of all variant frequencies and authentication of Muhammad's original oral wording remain outside this audit.

**Verdict:** absolute early lexical uniformity does not survive the documented comparisons. The evidence does not establish which form Muhammad originally recited or that every difference was intentional.
