# CI-014 Parisino-petropolitanus evidence audit - v0.63.0

## Witness and sources

- [Corpus Coranicum, BnF Arabe 328(a), f. 10v](https://corpuscoranicum.org/en/manuscripts/13/page/10v?sura=4&verse=11): catalogue and current line transcription, 56 folios, parchment, Hijazi I, Fustat provenance, estimated date 640-680 CE. No carbon dating result is listed. The estimate must not be transferred to a correction.
- [Yasin Dutton (2001), An Early Mushaf According to the Reading of Ibn Amir](https://doi.org/10.3366/jqs.2001.3.1.71), JQS 3(1), pp. 71-89, especially pp. 72-74. Full article text was accessible through a reproduced journal copy. Page-rendering attempts failed. Dutton uses a/b for recto/verso; comparisons here use r/v.
- [François Déroche (2014), Qurans of the Umayyads](https://kutub.nur.nu/English/Qurans-of-the-Umayyads-A-First-Overview_by-Deroche_Brill.pdf), chapter 1, pp. 30-32, notes 63-76. Extracted book text was inspected; page-rendering attempts failed. Note 68 locates 3:189 on f. 9a; note 72 refers to the 2009 study and Powers' discussion of 4:12.
- [Déroche (2021), The One and the Many](https://www.jstor.org/stable/j.ctv270kvh1), pp. 164-165, for the remaining uncertainty about 4:12. The relevant reproduced book text was accessible.
- [BnF catalogue of Déroche's 2009 edition](https://catalogue.bnf.fr/ark:/12148/cb42027745r). The catalogue establishes publication of the complete transcription, not independent inspection of its entire contents.

This audit does not identify Birmingham 1572a (Arabe 328(c)) or Marcel 2 as part of the Parisino-petropolitanus. The Parisino loci are one codex, not independent copies. Several requested Corpus Coranicum folio pages could not be opened directly; comparisons are attributed to the published studies. Normalised Arabic is not a diplomatic transcription.

## Historical verdict and falsification

Regional consonantal differences and corrections establish early textual non-identity. They are compatible with common written ancestry. The material alone does not determine whether every alternative originated with Muhammad or whether a particular correction was intentional suppression rather than ordinary copying intervention.

At 4:12 the pronoun warrants examination of the earlier legal context, but no different inheritance scheme can be derived without the remaining wording. New images, independent early parallels and securely dated interventions would change the assessment. Comparison with the Sanaa legal-list difference and the unreadable Marcel 2 erasure keeps these distinct evidence classes separate.

Next priority: other Marcel manuscripts with individually identified loci, recoverable readings and explicit correction states. No additional Marcel variant is claimed in this release.
