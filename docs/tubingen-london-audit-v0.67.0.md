# Tübingen and London manuscript evidence - v0.67.0

## Source access

- [Corpus Coranicum, Ma VI 165, f. 1r](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts/107/page/1r): indexed line transcription (Salome Beridze) and catalogue. Direct page opening returned a JavaScript shell. Line 14 has the second-person form at 17:43.
- [Marx and Jocham (2019)](https://corpuscoranicum.de/pdf/Radiocarbon_Dating_of_Quran_Manuscripts.pdf), Fig. 6.10, Table 6.2: 649-675 CE, 95.4%, combined parchment interval for three samples. PDF extracted text and figure description accessible; screenshot failed.
- [Fedeli, Carolin Kinne-Wall and Sidky (2025)](https://books.openbookpublishers.com/10.11647/obp.0498.09.pdf), pp. 424-425: two divergent vocalisation marks. Extracted chapter text consulted; screenshot failed. The reported readings refer to dotting and later vocalisation, not a reconstruction of the first scribe's voice.
- [Öztürk (2023)](https://doi.org/10.17120/omuifd.1353957), pp. 503-509: extracted Turkish text consulted; screenshot failed. The conclusion of broad continuity explicitly excepts copying errors.
- [Van Putten (2020)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-royal-asiatic-society/article/hisams-ibraham-evidence-for-a-canonical-quranic-reading-based-on-the-rasm/E1E00FF676696F869570F4A70C3115E4): full article text, Tübingen and Or. 2165 subsections, Fig. 4, Table 8 and regional analysis; figure itself was not independently collated.
- [British Library Or 2165](https://searcharchives.bl.uk/catalog/032-003309717): institutional record, 121 folios, eighth century. [Corpus Coranicum](https://corpuscoranicum.org/en/manuscripts/12/page/112v) lists 640-680 CE in the indexed .org entry; the [indexed .de entry](https://corpuscoranicum.de/en/manuscripts/12/page/28r) lists 750. Neither lists a radiocarbon determination. These are incompatible estimates rather than independent measurements.
- [Dutton (2004)](https://doi.org/10.3366/jqs.2004.6.1.43): abstract only. Syrian textual affiliation and verse divisions nearest Hims. Full article required before further specific folio readings can be attributed to it.
- [Islamic Awareness folio-content index](https://www.islamic-awareness.org/quran/text/mss/ms2165): secondary locator, not a source establishing the reading itself.

The 19:41 comparison combines the published correspondence with a verse-specific identification of Hisham's reading in the Tübingen subsection and the folio index. It is explicitly an inference. An image or transcription of the London leaf would confirm or challenge it. The excluded damaged line at 43:26 is not counted as a readable earlier variant. No claim is made that the two dispersed portions of Or. 2165 and Arabe 328(e) are independent copies.

## Verdict

Distinguish differences in written skeleton, pointing and vocalisation. The name variants concern the same person; the grammatical variations concern address and mood rather than a separate doctrine. The manuscript evidence does not establish uniform oral transmission at every locus or exact prophetic authorisation of the later readings. It also does not establish wholesale replacement of the early text.

No original object or complete facsimile was collated. Next: direct London folio collation, further Sanaa lower-text loci and better manuscript image access.
