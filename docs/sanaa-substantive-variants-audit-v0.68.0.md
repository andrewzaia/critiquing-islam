## Sources and method

- [Sadeghi and Bergmann (2010)](https://doi.org/10.1163/157005810X504518), Arabica 57, 343-436, Table 2 and Appendix 2 (pp. 424-431). The [reproduced author-annotated PDF](https://bible-quran.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/01/Sadeghi-Bergmann-The-Codex-of-a-Companion-of-the-Prophet-and-the-quran-of-the-Prophet.pdf) and accessible extracted comparison tables were consulted. Requested PDF screenshots failed; marginal corrections were not independently inspected visually.
- [Sadeghi and Goudarzi (2012)](https://doi.org/10.1515/islam-2011-0025), Der Islam 87, 1-129. The [reproduced article text](https://pdfcoffee.com/sanaa-1-and-the-origins-of-the-qurx27an-pdf-free.html) was consulted, particularly Table 2 (p. 25), Table 3 (pp. 37-38) and the folio transcription (pp. 44-50). Lower-text readings contain editorial brackets, parentheses, gaps and probable readings. Modern comparison snippets do not reproduce all those diplomatic conventions. Uncertainty is explicitly labelled where it affects the claim.
- [Corpus Coranicum, David 86/2003-I](https://corpuscoranicum.org/en/manuscripts/427/page/1v) identifies the lower-script folio and supplies a catalogue estimate of 640-680 CE, not a radiocarbon result. Recto/verso labels in this release follow the critical edition.

No manuscript images or original objects were independently collated. The published readings are attributed to their editors. All selected leaves belong to the same lower codex, not independent textual witnesses.

## Order and historical judgement

The 2012 Table 2 lists eleven surviving chapter transitions. Examples include 11 to 8, 9 to 19 and 62 to 89. The transition of 8 to 9 is also present without a basmala, as in the standard sequence. This is local sequence evidence and not an independently recovered complete arrangement. No chapter-order event is counted as another word-level catalogue entry.

Sanaa's non-standard text and chapter sequence challenge universal exact uniformity. Direction of change is a separate problem. The 2010 study favours the standard wording as closer to a shared prototype on its selected evidence and considers selection among companion copies as an alternative model. Its polarity analysis is not direct proof of every original word; the earlier physical layer does not automatically have the earlier lexical reading.

Independent parallels and better images could confirm or revise these findings. In particular, target David 2:218 and 2:222 and Bonhams 5:42 before strengthening the claims. No full-text frequency or percentage of variation is inferred from the selected records.
