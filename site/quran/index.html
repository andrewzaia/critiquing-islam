COVERAGE AND ATTRIBUTION

What do these differences represent?

Qirāʾa vs riwāya

The reading is attributed to a reader, with two named transmissions per reader in this dataset. A different typeface, translation or recording is not automatically another qirāʾa.

What is available

6,236 reference verses, seven canonical readers and fourteen transmissions. Abū Jaʿfar, Yaʿqūb and Khalaf al-ʿĀshir, along with their transmission lines, are not included. The two incomplete source-CSV word positions are explicitly marked.

Interpretation limits

A difference may involve pronunciation, vowel pattern or morphology and is not automatically a difference in meaning. No recorded variant does not establish that two complete oral performances are identical.

The English translation used in the reference verse and search results is Marmaduke Pickthall (1930), hosted locally from Quran JSON's English text under CC BY-SA 4.0; Pickthall's translation is public domain. Translation wording does not establish the meaning of other canonical readings.

Data source: Corpus Coranicum, adapted through Abdul Rahim Nizamani's dataset based on al-Dānī's at-Taysīr. Derived data are hosted locally under CC BY-SA 4.0. The Quran Foundation Qira'at API covers additional variation but requires approved production access.
