CI-014: Uthman and authenticity

Reading the evidence: wording is normalised from published descriptions, not a new diplomatic transcription. Dates concern parchment or estimated manuscript production, not the timing of a correction. Unreadable earlier wording is shown as unknown; these records do not establish which reading Muhammad originally recited.
