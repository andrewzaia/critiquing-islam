13 records have inferred, partial or unknown readings. A published reading is an attributed report, not an independently verified facsimile reading. A theological or legal context does not establish a changed doctrine or law. Canonical status is assessed only where the cited analysis identifies a parallel; "not established" is not a claim of exclusion.

How to interpret these classifications

Spelling / name form: same referent, including spelling correlated with pronunciation. Limited effect: local grammatical repair, emphasis, explicit referent or broadly equivalent wording, with no materially different proposition established. Meaning differs: a local lexical, grammatical or referent difference, not necessarily a new doctrine or rule. Impact unresolved: uncertain wording or insufficient context to determine the effect. An inferred reading may still have an assessable conditional impact. Physical status refers to reported manuscript intervention, not its date or motive. No intervention established is not proof that the writing was never altered.
