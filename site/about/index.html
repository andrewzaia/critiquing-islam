ABOUT / THE PROJECT

Biblical. Critical. Accountable.

A biblical-Christian critique of Islamic scripture and claims of divine authority, grounded in original texts, historical witnesses and transparent evidence standards.

Our perspective is stated openly.

The Bible provides the theological framework for comparing religious claims, while criticism of the Quran begins with its actual wording and context. Other evidence may establish historical facts or document how a community later interpreted a passage, but commentary cannot rewrite the wording being examined or provide independent verification of an otherwise unsupported claim.

Some claims can be tested directly against surviving texts. Others depend on contested history or disputed translations. We make those distinctions visible rather than allowing a later explanation to masquerade as a primary source.

Our Christian standpoint is explicit rather than disguised as religious neutrality. The goal is to test Islamic claims rigorously, without suppressing source evidence or predetermining historical findings. It is to publish clear, checkable findings and improve them when stronger evidence becomes available.
